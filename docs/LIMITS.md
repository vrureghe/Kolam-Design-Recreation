**What this models:** pulli (dot-grid) kolams whose curves wind around the dots on a *square* lattice
(sikku / kambi style). Every design is a choice of cross / bounce at each gate between two dots.

**What it does not model:** freehand/floral kolams without dots, hexagonal or triangular dot lattices
(detected and reported, but not analysed or generated), colour rangoli, 3-D or perspective photos.

**Detected vs inferred:** pixel measurements are *detected*; gate states, loop count and symmetry group
are *inferred* from a model and can be wrong if the dot lattice is wrong.

**Validation status:** tested on synthetic images only; see README. Real photographs are untested.
